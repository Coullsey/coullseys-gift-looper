Questions

Does it work on Mac?

Not at the moment. Gift Looper is a Windows app (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit).

Will the video have a transparent background?

Yes. Exports are WebM videos with a see-through background, so only your gifts and text show on stream.

Do I need to log in to TikTok?

No. Gift Looper never asks for your TikTok login. It only loads the public gift list and pictures.

How does my licence key work?

Straight after you subscribe you'll see your licence key. Open Gift Looper, paste it in and press Activate. A key works on one PC at a time. To move to a new PC, open Settings → Licence → "Remove from this PC" on the old one, or email us.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your subscription page. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions when it opens. When an update is ready, a button appears to restart and install it.
