Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information Coullsey's Gift Looper ("the app") and this website collect, and how it's used. We keep it to the minimum needed to sell and run the app.

When you buy

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your payment details; we never see your full card details. We receive your name, email address, country and order details so we can provide your licence and support. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy applies to the information they collect.

When you use the app

  • Your projects stay on your PC. Gifts, images, fonts and settings you add are saved on your computer and are not uploaded to us.
  • Licence checks. When you activate the app and when it starts, your licence key and an anonymous identifier for your PC are sent to our licence server to confirm your licence is valid.
  • Updates. The app contacts GitHub to check for and download updates.
  • TikTok gifts. When you use the TikTok gift picker, the app downloads the public gift list and gift pictures from TikTok's servers. It never asks for your TikTok login.

The app does not include advertising or tracking.

This website

This website doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; those services may log basic technical information such as your IP address under their own policies.

How we use your information

We only use your information to provide and support the app, manage your licence, and handle payments and refunds. We don't sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing coullseyslivetools@gmail.com. We may need to keep some order records where the law requires it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
